# Stratagem icons

These icons are hand traced from the HELLDIVERS™ 2 game assets by
[Dogo314](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/User:Dogo314) for the
[Helldivers Wiki](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Stratagems), and converted to 128x128 PNG files.

The original artwork belongs to [Arrowhead Game Studios](https://www.arrowheadgamestudios.com/) and its licensors,
under the [HELLDIVERS™ EULA](https://store.steampowered.com/eula/553850_eula_0).
All trademarks and registered trademarks are their property.

The tracings may only be used in free and publicly accessible content, with attribution to the uploader and
to the Helldivers Wiki. They are regenerated with `go run ./cmd/update-stratagems`.
